The game

Ozymandosis is free to play. An account is needed only for online play.

Membership

Membership costs $1 (USD) per month plus any applicable tax, renews monthly, and removes the time limit on online matches. Cancel any time from your account; it stays active until the end of the paid period.

Community rules

  • No harassment, hate speech, threats or sexual content, in names or chat.
  • No cheating, exploiting bugs against other players, or tampering with other players’ games.
  • No impersonating staff.

Moderators may warn, mute, suspend or ban accounts that break these rules. You can report players from the game.

Availability

Online services may change or pause for maintenance. Matches in progress run between players and usually continue if our servers go down.
