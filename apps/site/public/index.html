Six cultures, six ways to live

Each culture builds differently. Their nests, spires and buds are organisms too: they breathe, beat and grow the same organs as your creatures, and they show their wounds.

The Verdant Strain

Economy

Patient gardeners whose nests put down roots toward the nearest pools and drink.

The Luminants

Evolution

Keepers of the gold spore. Their halls wear nautilus shells and watch you with stalked eyes.

The Current-born

Speed and raids

Riders of the long water. Even their buildings seem to swim, trailing flagella in a vortex.

The Deep Choir

Sensing and ambush

Listeners in the dark. Their bells sing rings into the water and hear everything.

The Umbral Kin

Parasitism

The tethered hunger. A toothed maw, a cluster of eyes, and hooks that reach for whatever comes near.

The Bloomtide

Aggression

Brief and burning. Carapace, spikes and egg sacs that swell and burst into swarms.

Built to be lived in

Design your creatures

Thirty organs across legs, flagella, pili, mandibles and antennae. Combine them in the Spawnforge; every organ changes how a creature looks and fights.

A score that listens

The soundtrack is composed as you play: each culture has its own theme and mode, and the music swells from drifting ambience to pounding pulse as the fighting grows.

Readable by design

Hyperlegible type for dyslexic players, shape markers so colour is never the only signal, scalable interface, and controls for touch, mouse and keyboard (WASD included).

Plays anywhere

WebGL2 and WebGPU when your device has them, a lighter renderer when it does not, and a frame-budget governor that keeps phones smooth.

Online, directly between players

Our servers only introduce players: matchmaking, lobbies and ratings. The match itself runs directly between your devices over an encrypted connection, so it never passes through us. You can choose to hide your IP address from other players at the cost of a little delay.
