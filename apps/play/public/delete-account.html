Delete your account

How to erase your Ozymandosis account, and what happens to the data that belongs to it.

Delete it yourself, now

Sign in at your account and choose Delete my account. It takes effect immediately, without contacting anyone.

If you cannot sign in

Write to privacy@ozymandosis.com from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my Ozymandosis account. We action it and reply to confirm.

Include your display name if you still know it. It helps us find the right account, and it is the only thing we ask for.

What is erased

  • Your email address
  • Your sign-in identities from Google, Apple or Steam
  • Your password hash and your two-factor secret
  • Your display name, your rating, and the match results attributed to you
  • Your game progress: lineage, Spawnforge designs and save slots
  • The store's purchase reference for your membership
  • The reports you sent, and any moderation notes attached to them

What remains

Anonymised match records — who played whom, and who won — stay, with nothing left that points back to you. Those matches are already shared with everyone who played them, so removing your name does not erase the game.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Memberships are billed by the store you bought them in, so cancel it there — Google Play, the App Store or Steam. Deleting the account stops you signing in; it does not stop the store charging you.
