First public build.

- Online play: one free match every 24 hours, then unlimited with a membership bought on
  the website.
- Local network play with no account and no internet.
- Campaign, skirmish and the Spawnforge; five rival cultures.
- Fixed since the playtest: joining a game by its code, sound returning after the app is
  backgrounded, and the Android build no longer confusing its own address for a
  development server.
