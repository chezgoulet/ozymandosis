A real-time strategy game of living light.

You tend a glowing culture on a living world: harvest drifting lumen, evolve your
creatures organ by organ in the Spawnforge, and send living swarms against rival
cultures. Everything about the colony shows in its colour — fever, starvation and
blight are visible on the board, not buried in a panel.

Every planet, creature, structure and note of music is generated from code: there are no
downloaded assets and no samples. The music adapts to the battle as it turns.

Online play needs an account. One full match every 24 hours is free — hosting or joining,
however long it runs. A membership makes online play unlimited, and it is bought on our
website, not inside this app: this build has no payment code in it at all. Local network
play is always free and needs no account or internet.

This is the same app that ships on Google Play, built from the same source, with the
store's billing library removed.
