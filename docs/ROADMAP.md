# Roadmap

**This is a wishlist.** Everything below is an idea under consideration, not a
promise. There are no dates, because a date would turn it into a promise and I
would rather tell you the truth than miss a deadline I invented. This page will
change, and the changes are the point.

## What you get for $1

The game, complete. **Every feature on this page will arrive as a free update to
anyone who owns it** — no DLC, no season pass, no content held back, nothing
reserved for a better edition.

One thing costs money, and it is not content. **One multiplayer match a day is
free, every day, of any length and any kind** — starting it or joining someone
else's makes no difference. If you want more than that, a subscription buys
unlimited matches. Nothing else in the game is ever metered.

## Now

Making the core stable, good and fun, and getting it onto the machines people
actually own: **Android first, then iOS, then Steam.**

## Ideas under consideration

**Behemoths** *(working name)* — a configurable world event. Colossal organisms
that hunt the creatures on the map and can turn the course of a game. Several
kinds, each with its own way of ruining your afternoon.

**More to build** — more colony structures, with their own buffs, drawbacks and
unlockable abilities; and more organs for your creatures, in the same
five-class, six-form, four-tier grid.

**More ways to fight** — poison clouds, bio bombs, mines, constricting tendrils,
draining bites, and parasites that cling to a structure and drink its lumen.

**A richer world** — more varied terrain, and hazards and opportunities worth
fighting over rather than scenery to cross.

**A story** — a campaign: missions with objectives of their own, telling one
tale from beginning to end.

**Together** — leaderboards, somewhere to talk to each other, and a free month of
multiplayer for both of you when a friend joins on your recommendation.

---

*Nothing here is scheduled. Everything here is included in the game you already
bought.*
